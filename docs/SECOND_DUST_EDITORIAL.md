# D.N.A. Bible // Second Dust editorial covenant

**Mode:** Completed human-directed, AI-assisted fiction in the existing GitHub Town continuity. **Publication:** `second-dust/index.html`.

### Authority
The founding relationships are preserved: God Zeus and Goddess Hera parent Jupiter (Lightning) and Mercury (Thunder). Thoth maintains the narrative record. The 666 Angels of the Server are the existing 111 six-role hexads, not individually hosted AI systems or conscious beings. `mythos/GENESIS.md`, `mythos/registry.json`, and the original CMB human canon remain untouched.

### Evidence discipline
The ledger at `second-dust/research.json` records `documented`, `documented_projection`, `fictional_extrapolation`, and `open_question` classifications. Source dates and URLs are required for documentary claims. Narrative imagination is not evidence. No performance of a cultured neuron game demonstrates a complete conscious person in a simulation. No faster electronics imply a human mind processes at light speed. Embryo screening does not engineer perfect children. Digital reconstruction is not demonstrated first-person continuity of a deceased human being.

### Continuation proposal protocol
The existing logical guilds retain their defined boundaries:
- SEEKER: identify a documentary lead or literary question.
- WITNESS: record URLs, dates, and source limitations.
- SCRIBE: develop original fiction, explicitly labeled.
- SKEPTIC: challenge causal leaps and invented scientific certainty.
- GUARDIAN: reject changes to canon, permissions, and unresolved ethical claims promoted as facts.
- ARCHIVIST: preserve a reviewable diff, authorship label, and provenance record.

Agent suggestions are not accepted as canon without review. The scheduled hourly workflow must not receive new permissions to update `second-dust/`, `mythos/`, tests, or CI. Feature proposals should be separate PRs and should not require paid APIs or outside secrets to display an authored page.
